Lab edition · how this lab works

This page replaces Simulink and MATLAB for Lab 4. It simulates the mass-spring-damper system (Ex.1) and runs the PID controller on the Arduino Nano of the balance-beam (Ex.2) and ball-in-a-tube (Ex.3) rigs. The PID loop itself runs on the Nano every 0.1 s, and this page sends it the gains and the setpoint and plots what the Nano measures.

  1. Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. Other browsers cannot talk to the serial port.
  2. Ex.1 needs no hardware: open Ex.1 Simulation and start.
  3. For Ex.2 and Ex.3, connect the Nano by USB, switch on the 5 V bench supply, and press Connect to Arduino. In the list, choose the Nano's COM port (often USB-SERIAL CH340).
  4. Set the gains with the sliders or type the values. Every run is added to the current figure, like hold on in MATLAB. Each figure in the sub-tabs matches one plot of the answer sheet.
  5. Write your comments in the Report tab and press Print / Save as PDF. The figures are inserted automatically.

Your runs and answers are kept in this browser until you press "Clear everything" in the Report tab.
