Study edition · practise PID control at home

Use this edition to prepare for Lab 4. It does not produce the lab report. The lab itself uses the lab edition on the lab PCs, with the real rigs.

  1. Ex.1 Simulation: choose a system (mass-spring-damper, first or second order, with delay, an integrator, third order) and move the P, I and D sliders. The dotted curve follows the sliders live. Press "Add to figure" to keep a curve and compare.
  2. Ex.2 and Ex.3: press Use emulator to rehearse the rig procedure with simulated rigs. Tick "Apply slider changes during the run" to retune the controller while it runs.
  3. Self-test: questions on other systems, with feedback. Try each one in the simulator before answering.
The emulator uses models identified on the lab rigs. It reproduces their behaviour only roughly, so expect different numbers on the real rigs.
